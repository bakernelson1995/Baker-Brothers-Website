Our story

Meet the Baker Bros

Two brothers, a lifetime on the mat, and one goal: helping wrestlers reach their potential.

Coach

Nelson Baker

A lifelong student of the sport, Nelson wrestled NCAA Division III at the University of Wisconsin-La Crosse. He has served as head coach of the 16U Team Illinois Greco-Roman dual team and was voted IWCOA Assistant Coach of the Year. He's now fully committed to developing Freestyle and Greco-Roman wrestlers, with folkstyle largely in the rearview.

  • 2x IHSA State Champion
  • 3x Greco-Roman All-American
  • Team Illinois National Coach
Coach

Nolan Baker

A Byron HS alum, Nolan wrestled Division I at Northern Illinois University and trained as a resident athlete at the U.S. Olympic Training Center in Colorado Springs. He's competed and trained internationally in Greco-Roman wrestling across Norway, Sweden, Romania, Georgia and beyond, giving him a rare mix of northern Illinois roots and world-level experience to bring to every athlete he coaches.

  • 2x State Champion
  • U23 Greco-Roman World Team Member
  • Greco-Roman National Champion
  • 6x Greco-Roman All-American
  • Team Illinois National Coach
What we believe

Our coaching philosophy

Details win

Close matches are decided by hand position, angles and timing. We teach the small things most coaches skip.

Earn it live

A move isn't learned until it works against resistance. Every session ends with live, pressure-tested reps.

Build the person

Discipline, accountability and toughness carry far beyond the mat. We coach the athlete and the person.
